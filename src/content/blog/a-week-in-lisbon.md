I went to Lisbon with no real plan, which turned out to be the best decision of the trip.

## Slow Mornings

Coffee, a pastel de nata, and no agenda. I let the city set the pace instead of the other way around.

## Walking Everywhere

Lisbon rewards wandering. Every hill hides a viewpoint, and the tiled facades make even a wrong turn feel worthwhile.

## What I'm Bringing Home

Less rushing. More noticing. A reminder that the point of travel isn't to see everything — it's to be somewhere fully.
