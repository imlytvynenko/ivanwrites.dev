I'm not fast. I never have been. But I show up, and over time that turned out to matter more than any single workout.

## Showing Up Beats Intensity

The runs that built my base weren't the hard ones — they were the easy ones I did anyway, on the days I didn't feel like it.

## Small Loops Compound

A short loop before work seemed too small to matter. A year later, those small loops added up to hundreds of miles and a much clearer head.

## The Same Rule Applies Everywhere

Writing, coding, learning a language — consistency quietly outperforms motivation. Running just taught me to trust it.
